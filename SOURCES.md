# Official sources and content verification

This MVP links to official government portals and uses summaries of selected public guidance. Re-check every service page before public release.

- Government of Kenya portal: https://gok.kenya.go.ke/
- eCitizen: https://ecitizen.go.ke/
- Huduma Kenya service directory and centres: https://www.hudumakenya.go.ke/
- Directorate of Immigration application requirements: https://immigration.go.ke/application-requirements/
- Directorate of Immigration passport information: https://immigration.go.ke/passport-section/
- Civil Registration Services: https://crs.ecitizen.go.ke/
- NTSA eCitizen services: https://ntsa.ecitizen.go.ke/
- KRA: https://www.kra.go.ke/ and https://itax.kra.go.ke/
- Business Registration Service: https://brs.go.ke/
- Ministry of Foreign and Diaspora Affairs eCitizen: https://mfa.ecitizen.go.ke/
- Directorate of Criminal Investigations: https://dci.go.ke/
- Kenya eTA: https://www.etakenya.go.ke/

## Content policy
- Never describe this as a government-owned or government-approved app.
- Never invent fees, processing times, office addresses, or required documents.
- Where a checklist is marked “Verify”, the app deliberately asks users to confirm the case-specific list with the responsible agency.
- Keep the date checked, source URL, reviewer, and change notes for each service when maintaining this dataset.
- Do not scrape protected pages or bypass authentication. Prefer official published information and permissioned datasets.
